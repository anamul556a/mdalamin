FAQ

Everything you want to know before you order.

Straight answers about LLMO, GEO, AIO, AEO, AI SEO, Safety, Results and how the work is done.

What are GEO, AEO and LLMO?

GEO (Generative Engine Optimization), AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) and LLMO (Large Language Model Optimization) all mean one thing: making your brand easy for AI tools like ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Perplexity to understand, trust and cite. It covers entity clarity, structured data, answer-ready content and brand mentions.

How is GEO different from traditional SEO?

SEO helps you rank in a list of links. GEO helps AI engines pick your brand as the answer and cite it as a source. GEO uses SEO as its base, then adds entity signals, schema, AI citations and content written so a language model can quote it.

Do I still need normal SEO if I focus on AI search?

Yes. AI engines rely on pages that are crawlable, indexed, fast and trusted. That is why every package includes deep on-page and technical SEO along with GEO, AEO and LLMO, so you grow in Google and in AI answers together.

Which AI platforms do you optimize for?

ChatGPT Search, Google AI Overviews, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude, Microsoft Copilot and voice assistants. The work is built on signals all of them share: clear entities, structured data, trusted mentions and answer-ready content.

Is it safe for my website?

Yes. I use white-hat methods only and follow Google guidelines. No spam, no PBN tricks, no keyword stuffing. Every link and citation is placed on relevant sites with natural anchors.

Can you guarantee a #1 ranking or AI citations?

No, and be careful with anyone who does. Nobody controls Google or what an AI engine says. What I guarantee is quality work, a clear plan, full transparency and unlimited revisions on everything I deliver.

How long until I see results?

Technical fixes, schema and on-page updates can start showing once Google re-crawls your pages, often within days to a few weeks. Authority signals such as citations, PR and backlinks build over time, so most sites see the bigger movement over the following 2 to 3 months. Competitive niches can take longer.

How do you measure AI visibility?

Your final report shows before and after: indexing and GSC status, schema validation, ranking movement for your target keywords, and checks of your target questions across AI engines to see if your brand is mentioned or cited. AI answers change often, so I report what was observed, not promises.

What are AI citations?

AI citations are brand and URL mentions on trusted, relevant sites and platforms that AI engines read and reference. The more consistent, credible mentions your brand has, the easier it is for AI engines to recognize and recommend you.

What does the PR in GEO Build and GEO Authority mean?

Digital PR placements: coverage of your brand on relevant publications and websites. They build real authority and trust signals that help both Google and AI engines see you as a credible source.
